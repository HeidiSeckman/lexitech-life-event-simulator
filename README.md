# 🌪️ LexiTech — Worst-Case Life Event Contract Simulator (Module 03)
### Adversarial Crisis Stress-Testing Engine by Heidi Seckman

🌐 **LexiTech Studio Headquarters:** https://github.io

## 🚀 Overview
Most contracts are drafted assuming everything goes smoothly—until a sudden real-world emergency turns standard boilerplate into a devastating financial trap. **Module 03** stress-tests any private contract (residential/commercial leases, employment agreements, partnership deals, or service contracts) against worst-case life events to expose hidden liabilities before you sign.

## ⚡ Key Capabilities
- **Adversarial Scenario Stress-Testing:** Simulates **Sudden Job Loss / Layoff**, **Medical Emergency / Disability**, **Business Partner Ghosting or Default**, **Divorce / Household Split**, and **Natural Disaster / Force Majeure**.
- **Closed-Book Grounding:** Anchors every financial penalty, acceleration clause, and personal guarantee strictly to exact sections and page numbers in the uploaded PDF.
- **3 Executive Audit Views:**
  1. 🚨 **Crisis Impact & Exposure Matrix** (clause-by-clause financial & legal fallout)
  2. 🛡️ **Pre-Signing Protection Clauses** (custom escape hatches, hardship waivers & redlines)
  3. 📋 **Emergency Survival Action Plan** (step-by-step checklist & downloadable `.txt` memo)
- **Built-In Simulation Sandbox:** One-click sample lease & employment trap contract for instant testing.
